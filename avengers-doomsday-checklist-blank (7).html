  6. Avengers: Endgame (2019): Steve's ending and the time heist; watch the 2026 Encore cut.

  12. Deadpool & Wolverine (2024): Tatum's Gambit, the TVA, the Void: the Fox-to-MCU bridge.
  13. Captain America: Brave New World (2025): Sam Wilson's first film as Captain America.
  14. Thunderbolts* (2025): Yelena's team becomes the New Avengers; Sentry debuts.

Short on time?

If you only have a weekend, prioritize Endgame: Encore, Loki S1–2, Multiverse of Madness, Deadpool & Wolverine, Thunderbolts*, First Steps and Brand New Day.

Notes and exclusions

  • Dates are first US release (streaming premiere for series); multi-season shows are grouped under their first season's date.
  • Endgame: Encore opened Sep 25, 2026 (some outlets list Sep 24 previews); its new scenes sit at the very end and in the credits.
  • Not listed: pre-1992 and kids' TV (1977 Incredible Hulk, 1967/1994 Spider-Man cartoons, Spidey and His Amazing Friends, Moon Girl and Devil Dinosaur), TV pilots (Nick Fury) and Marvel One-Shots. None matter for Doomsday.
  • Coming after Doomsday: Your Friendly Neighborhood Spider-Man S2 (Jan 13, 2027), Marvel Zombies specials (2027), Daredevil: Born Again S3 (2027).
